# Lessons Learned

## Observation

Initial investigation began as a perceived controller defect.

## Finding

The observed behavior was not caused by hardware failure.

The behavior resulted from differing assumptions regarding avionics architecture.

## Systems Engineering Lesson

Architectural assumptions must be explicitly identified during requirements development.

## Human Factors Lesson

Mode awareness and operational parity significantly influence usability.

## Design Lesson

Integrated and independent avionics architectures require different control abstractions.
