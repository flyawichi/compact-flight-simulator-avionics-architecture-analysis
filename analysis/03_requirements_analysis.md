# Preliminary Requirement Recommendation

## REQ-001

The controller shall support both Integrated Avionics Mode and Independent Navigator Mode.

## REQ-002

When operating in Independent Navigator Mode, all FMS2 functions shall provide operational parity with equivalent FMS1 functions.

## REQ-003

The controller shall provide independent state indication for Navigator 1 and Navigator 2 operational modes.

## REQ-004

The controller shall provide visual annunciation when Navigator 2 enters cursor or map manipulation modes.
